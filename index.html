Wonmin Kim

Electrical and Computer Engineering · University of California San Diego

I work on machine learning for biological imaging at UC San Diego, together with Johannes Schöneberg. My research asks what image models should learn from microscopy of cells — how to build Cell Painting representations that retrieve a perturbation's effect across laboratories rather than the lab it came from, and how self-supervised models of live-cell mitochondrial images can read donor age and drug response.

Wonmin Kim, Johannes Schöneberg

A 21.7 M-parameter ViT-S student, taught by CellProfiler consensus profiles and frozen DINOv2 features, outperforms 14× larger frozen models on held-out JUMP laboratories and complements CellProfiler in fusion.

Research · 2026 · Co-first author

Representation learning on live-cell mitochondrial images defines an aging axis and age-dependent drug responses in primary human fibroblasts

Elisavetta Ilyin*, Wonmin Kim*, Johannes Schöneberg

A self-supervised encoder trained on 144,624 living fibroblasts, without age or compound labels, reads donor age from mitochondria and shows that the same drug acts differently on adult and neonatal cells.
