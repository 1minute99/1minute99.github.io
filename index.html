M.S. Student · Electrical and Computer Engineering · UC San Diego

I'm an M.S. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at UC San Diego, working with Prof. Johannes Schöneberg. I develop AI for biology and drug discovery: machine learning models that read how cells respond to drugs from microscopy images, and that keep working when the lab, batch or donor changes.

My work so far shows that a small image model taught by the right teachers can outperform frozen models up to 14× its size, including Cell Painting foundation models, at retrieving a compound's effect across laboratories; and that a self-supervised model of live-cell mitochondria, trained without age or drug labels, can read donor age and reveal that drugs act differently on adult and neonatal cells. In my PhD, I want to develop AI that learns from large-scale biological data to understand how cells respond to drugs and to help discover new therapies.

I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.
