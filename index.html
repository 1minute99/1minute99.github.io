Wonmin Kim

Electrical and Computer Engineering · University of California San Diego

I work on machine learning at UC San Diego, focusing on representation learning: what a model should be taught so that its features keep the signal that matters and drop the nuisance that doesn't. I approach this through self-supervised learning, knowledge distillation and robustness to distribution shift. With Johannes Schöneberg, I currently apply these ideas to microscopy of cells — retrieving a perturbation's effect across laboratories, and reading donor age and drug response from live-cell images.

Research · 2026 · Co-first author

Representation learning on live-cell mitochondrial images defines an aging axis and age-dependent drug responses in primary human fibroblasts

Elisavetta Ilyin*, Wonmin Kim*, Johannes Schöneberg

A self-supervised encoder trained on 144,624 living fibroblasts, without age or compound labels, reads donor age from mitochondria and shows that the same drug acts differently on adult and neonatal cells.
