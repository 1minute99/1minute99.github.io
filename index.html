Wonmin Kim

Electrical and Computer Engineering · University of California San Diego

I work on machine learning for biological imaging at UC San Diego, together with Johannes Schöneberg. My research asks what image models should be taught to describe cell morphology — in particular, how to build Cell Painting representations that retrieve a perturbation's effect across laboratories, rather than the lab, batch or plate position an image came from.
